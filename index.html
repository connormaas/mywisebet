        Blackjack

        Beat the dealer

        Everyone at the table plays against the dealer, not against each other. Your coach explains every step and suggests the best move each time.

        The goalFinish closer to 21 than the dealer. Go over 21 and you bust, which loses right away.
        Card values2 to 10 count as their number. J, Q and K count as 10. An Ace counts as 1 or 11, whichever helps.
        Your movesHit takes a card. Stand stops. Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card. Split turns a pair into two hands.
        The dealerHas no choices: hits until 17 or more. Wins pay 1 to 1, a Blackjack (Ace plus a 10-value card) pays 3 to 2, and a tie is a push.

        Meet the table

        Learn by playing

        Welcome to the table

        This is No-Limit Texas Hold'em, the most popular kind of poker. You sit at the bottom seat with $1,000 in chips. Five other players join you. I deal, and your coach explains every step and suggests a move when it is your turn.

        The goalWin chips. Either have the best 5-card hand at the end, or make everyone else fold.
        The cardsYou get 2 private cards. Then 5 shared cards come out: 3 (flop), 1 (turn), 1 (river).
        The bettingA betting round happens before the flop and after each new card. Fold, check, call, or raise.
        The blindsThe two players left of the dealer button post forced bets ($5 and $10) so there is always a pot to fight for.

        Meet the table

        They change as the game goes: losing a big pot can put someone "on tilt" (playing angry and reckless), winning makes them bolder, and they adjust to how you play. Turn on X-ray in the More menu any time to see their cards and their reasoning.

        Optional: bomb pots

        A house rule many card rooms use for fun. Instead of blinds, everyone puts in $50 (an "ante"), there is no betting before the flop, and the flop is dealt right away. Every player sees it, so the pot starts big and hands get wild. Turn them on in the setup card or the More menu.

        About

        A practice table, not a casino

        My Wise Bet is a free, educational game for learning how poker and blackjack actually work: the rules, the odds, and how to make a good decision under uncertainty. Everything happens against computer players, with a coach explaining each step.

        No real moneyChips are pretend and reset any time. There are no deposits, purchases, prizes or payouts, ever.
        Built to teachWin chances, pot odds and basic strategy are shown on every turn so you learn why a move is good, not just what to click.
        The honest lessonThe math on screen shows why casinos win over time. Blackjack and slots have a house edge, and luck evens out against you.
        PrivateNo accounts, ads or sign-ups. Anonymous usage stats (pages visited and the start of coach questions) help improve the game. Nothing is sold or shared.

        Playing here is practice, not a reason to gamble for real. If gambling stops being fun for you or someone you know, free, confidential help is available 24/7 in the US at 1-800-GAMBLER or ncpgambling.org.
