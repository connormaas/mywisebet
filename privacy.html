My Wise Bet
Privacy

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no cookies, and nothing is ever sold. A few anonymous stats help improve the game, and coach questions go to an AI service to get an answer.

Updated November 24, 2026

Usage stats

When you visit, the site records:

  • which page you opened, the site that sent you there, and how long you stayed
  • device and browser type, screen size, language, time zone and country

Your IP address is used only to make a visitor code that changes every day and can't be traced back to you. The IP address itself is not saved. Stats are deleted after 400 days.

The AI coach

When you ask the coach a question, your question, the recent chat and a description of the cards on the table are sent to Groq to write the answer. The first 120 characters of each question are saved with the usage stats so the coach can get better. Please don't type personal details into the chat.

To keep the free coach fair, each IP address can ask a limited number of questions per minute. That check happens in memory and isn't stored.

On your device

Your game settings (speed, seats, coach and log options) are saved in your browser so they're there next time. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Services that run the site

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Cloudflare runs the coach and the usage stats, and serves the 3D library.
  • Groq writes the coach's answers.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts.

Like any web server, these services see your IP address when your browser contacts them, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Children

My Wise Bet is made for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
